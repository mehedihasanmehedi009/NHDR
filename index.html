ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

Preserving The History Of Bangladesh's Liberation War

The 1971 Liberation War Archive is a digital platform dedicated to collecting, preserving and sharing the history of Bangladesh's struggle for independence.

From the historic 7 March speech to the final victory on 16 December 1971, our archive documents the events, people and sacrifices that shaped our nation.

Students, researchers and future generations can explore important historical documents, interviews, photographs and memories of the Liberation War.
